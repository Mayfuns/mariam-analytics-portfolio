The business question

VitalCare needed a clearer way to compare hospital performance across years and understand how operational activity, clinical outcomes and financial results were changing together.

The analysis was structured around a practical question: where is performance improving, where is pressure increasing, and which metrics require management attention?

Analytical approach

The project brings multiple hospital performance measures into a structured Power BI reporting model, using Power Query for preparation, a model designed around reusable dimensions and DAX measures for year-on-year comparison.

What the analysis covers

Admissions & OperationsAdmissions, patient activity, utilisation, length of stay and operational demand.
Clinical OutcomesMortality, readmissions and outcome-focused performance indicators.
Financial PerformanceRevenue, costs, profit and margin performance across the reporting period.
Department ComparisonDepartment-level performance to help identify variation and areas requiring attention.

Key findings

  • Revenue increased by 5.75% year on year, indicating stronger top-line financial performance.
  • Mortality decreased by 15.77%, showing improvement in this clinical outcome measure.
  • Readmissions increased by 8.68%, creating a clear area for further investigation and potential intervention.
  • Profit margin moved from 14.82% to 14.67%, suggesting that revenue growth did not translate into a stronger margin at the same rate.

Decision-support perspective

The value of the dashboard is not simply displaying KPIs. It places operational, clinical and financial measures together so decision-makers can see trade-offs. For example, revenue growth can be viewed alongside cost and margin performance, while improving mortality can be considered alongside the rise in readmissions.

This makes the analysis useful for prioritising follow-up questions rather than treating each metric in isolation.
