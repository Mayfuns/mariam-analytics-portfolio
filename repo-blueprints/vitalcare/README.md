# VitalCare Health Group — Healthcare Business Intelligence

A Power BI case study analysing hospital operations, clinical outcomes and financial performance across **2023 and 2024**.

[View the full portfolio case study](https://mayfuns.github.io/mariam-analytics-portfolio/vitalcare.html)

![VitalCare Power BI dashboard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mayfuns/mariam-analytics-portfolio/main/assets/images/vitalcare.png)

## Project overview

VitalCare needed a clearer way to compare hospital performance across years and understand how operational activity, clinical outcomes and financial results were changing together.

The project brings these measures into one structured Power BI reporting model so decision-makers can review performance, identify areas requiring attention and see trade-offs across different parts of the organisation.

## Business question

**How did hospital performance change from 2023 to 2024 across operations, clinical outcomes and financial performance, and which changes deserve further investigation?**

## Analysis areas

### Admissions & operations
Admissions, patient activity, utilisation, length of stay and operational demand.

### Clinical outcomes
Mortality, readmissions and outcome-focused performance indicators.

### Financial performance
Revenue, costs, profit and margin performance across the reporting period.

## Key findings

| KPI | Result | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Revenue | **+5.75% YoY** | Top-line financial performance improved in 2024. |
| Mortality | **−15.77% YoY** | This clinical outcome measure improved year on year. |
| Readmissions | **+8.68% YoY** | Readmissions increased and warrant further investigation. |
| Profit margin | **14.67% in 2024** | Margin declined slightly from **14.82%**, despite revenue growth. |

The combined view is important because no single KPI tells the full story. Revenue growth can be considered alongside cost and margin performance, while the improvement in mortality can be reviewed alongside the increase in readmissions.

## Analytical workflow

1. **Prepare and validate**  
   Cleaned and structured the source data in Power Query and checked the measures required for reporting.

2. **Model the data**  
   Built a reporting model designed around reusable dimensions and consistent analytical relationships.

3. **Build DAX measures**  
   Created measures for core KPIs and year-on-year comparisons.

4. **Communicate decisions**  
   Designed dashboard views that bring operational, clinical and financial performance into one decision-support experience.

## What this project demonstrates

- Translating a broad healthcare performance question into a structured BI solution.
- Combining operational, clinical and financial KPIs rather than analysing them in isolation.
- Using DAX to support comparative performance analysis.
- Building reporting with a clear decision-support purpose.
- Communicating both positive performance changes and areas requiring further investigation.
