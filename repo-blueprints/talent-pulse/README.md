# Talent Pulse Solutions — Workforce Analytics

A Tableau and Excel case study analysing employee attrition, retention risk, tenure and workforce segments across a dataset of **1,485 employees**.

[View the full portfolio case study](https://mayfuns.github.io/mariam-analytics-portfolio/talent-pulse.html)

![Talent Pulse workforce analytics dashboard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mayfuns/mariam-analytics-portfolio/main/assets/images/talent-pulse.png)

## Project overview

Talent Pulse needed a clearer view of employee attrition across departments, age groups, tenure and other workforce segments.

Rather than relying only on raw exit counts, the analysis compares attrition proportionally across workforce groups to identify where employee loss is concentrated and where retention action could have the greatest impact.

## Business question

**Where is attrition concentrated, which employee groups are most exposed to retention risk, and where should the business investigate first?**

## Analysis areas

### Department risk
Attrition rates were compared across business functions to identify where employee loss was disproportionately high.

### Tenure patterns
Employee exits were examined by years at the company, with particular attention to early-tenure risk.

### Age segmentation
Attrition patterns were assessed across age groups to reveal workforce segments requiring closer attention.

### Retention signals
Multiple workforce dimensions were brought together to support more targeted retention decisions.

## Key findings

| Measure | Result | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Workforce analysed | **1,485 employees** | Population included in the analysis. |
| Recorded exits | **243** | Employees classified as attrition cases. |
| Overall attrition | **16.4%** | Organisation-wide attrition rate. |
| Sales attrition | **20.54%** | Sales was one of the clearest department-level pressure points. |
| Age 18–26 attrition | **34.57%** | Younger employees showed elevated turnover. |
| First 2 years attrition | **30.43%** | Early tenure emerged as a major retention-risk period. |

The overall attrition rate is useful as a headline measure, but the more actionable insight comes from understanding **where** attrition is concentrated. The analysis shows that turnover risk is not evenly distributed across the workforce.

## Analytical workflow

1. **Prepare and validate**  
   Structured and checked the workforce data in Excel before analysis.

2. **Segment the workforce**  
   Organised employees into meaningful groups including department, age and tenure.

3. **Compare attrition rates**  
   Analysed attrition proportionally across segments rather than relying on raw counts alone.

4. **Communicate retention risk**  
   Built Tableau views to show where turnover is concentrated and where further investigation could be prioritised.

## Decision-support perspective

The analysis helps move the conversation beyond a single organisation-wide attrition rate.

By showing where turnover is concentrated, leaders can distinguish broad workforce issues from specific department, age or tenure-related patterns. This can support more targeted action such as:

- Strengthening onboarding and early-tenure support.
- Reviewing early-career development and engagement.
- Investigating retention challenges within high-risk departments.
- Comparing workforce groups before designing broad retention interventions.

## What this project demonstrates

- Turning a broad workforce problem into a structured analytical question.
- Using proportional analysis to compare groups fairly.
- Applying segmentation to identify concentrated retention risk.
- Combining Excel data preparation with Tableau visual analysis.
- Translating workforce metrics into practical decision-support insight.
