The business question

The purpose of MotivaAutos was to use SQL to turn structured automotive business data into clear answers that can support operational and commercial decision-making.

The focus was not simply writing queries, but using SQL to move from raw relational tables to understandable business information.

Analytical approach

The project follows a practical SQL workflow: understand the available tables, identify relationships, filter and combine relevant records, calculate summaries, and organise query outputs around business questions.

01Inspect tables
02Join related data
03Aggregate results
04Interpret findings

SQL capabilities demonstrated

Data RetrievalUsing SELECT, filtering and sorting to isolate relevant business records.
Relational JoinsConnecting related tables to build a fuller view of the underlying business data.
AggregationUsing grouped calculations to summarise performance and compare segments.
Business QuestioningStructuring queries around practical questions rather than producing isolated technical outputs.

What this project demonstrates

  • Ability to work with structured relational datasets and understand table relationships.
  • Use of SQL to cleanly retrieve, combine and summarise business information.
  • Translating analytical questions into reproducible queries.
  • Presenting query outputs in a way that supports interpretation and follow-up decisions.

Decision-support perspective

MotivaAutos shows SQL as more than a technical extraction tool. The queries are used to create a reliable analytical layer between raw operational data and the questions a business stakeholder may want answered.

This is the same principle used in larger BI workflows: build trustworthy logic first, then communicate the result clearly.
