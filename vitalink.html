The business question

VitalLink needed better visibility across patient alerts, clinician response and device performance. A key stakeholder concern was that device issues — particularly battery or connectivity problems — might be creating unnecessary warning alerts.

The analysis was structured around a practical question: what is driving alert overload, where is the 2-hour clinical response target being missed, and does the data support the assumption that device health is the main cause of warning-alert volume?

Analytical approach

The project used a star-schema-style model centred on the alert fact table, supported by patient, clinician and device dimensions. Tableau calculations were used to evaluate alert severity, response times, SLA compliance, workload and device-warning patterns.

Key findings

  • 10,500 alerts were generated, including 1,591 Critical alerts and 3,668 Warning alerts.
  • Overall SLA compliance was only 19.8%; Critical SLA compliance was stronger at 54.2%, but almost half of Critical cases still exceeded the 2-hour target.
  • North Hub performed best at 57.1% Critical SLA compliance, while West Hub was lowest at 51.6%.
  • Device-model warning rates were relatively close, ranging from 33.3% for GlucoTrack X to 37.0% for CelluPulse Pro.

Assumption tested: were the devices the problem?

The device investigation did not show a strong enough pattern to support a blanket device-failure explanation. Battery level showed little visible relationship with Warning Alert Rate, and Bluetooth and Cellular devices followed similar patterns.

The evidence pointed more strongly toward alert prioritisation, SLA monitoring and operational response as the areas requiring attention. Device health still warrants targeted investigation, but not broad replacement based on the available evidence.

Decision-support perspective

The analysis supports four practical actions: separate Critical alerts into a priority queue, monitor Critical SLA performance by hub and clinician in real time, review clinician workload alongside SLA performance, and investigate higher-warning device models selectively rather than assuming all devices are the root cause.

The broader value of the project is the analytical process: start with the stakeholder assumption, test it against multiple dimensions of the data, and redirect attention toward the evidence-supported operational issue.
