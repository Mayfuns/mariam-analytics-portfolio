The business question

An e-commerce retailer needs more than a list of transactions. The key challenge is understanding what is driving sales, which products contribute most, how performance changes over time and where commercial attention should be focused.

This project organises the data around those practical questions and turns the underlying records into a decision-ready Power BI view.

Analytical approach

The workflow focuses on preparing the source data, creating a clean analytical structure and building reusable measures that support interactive reporting across time, products and customer activity.

What the analysis covers

Sales PerformanceOverall commercial performance and how sales move across the reporting period.
Product AnalysisProduct and category contribution to help distinguish stronger and weaker performers.
Customer ActivityPatterns in purchasing behaviour that can support better commercial decisions.
Time-Based TrendsChanges across periods to make seasonality, growth and performance shifts easier to identify.

Decision-support perspective

The dashboard is designed to move from “what happened?” to “where should we look next?”. Bringing sales, products, customers and time-based patterns together makes it easier to identify areas that merit deeper investigation.

This type of reporting can support decisions around product focus, promotional planning, inventory attention and broader commercial performance monitoring.

Skills demonstrated

  • Transforming transactional data into business-focused reporting.
  • Structuring a Power BI model for repeatable analysis.
  • Creating KPI-led views rather than presenting raw records.
  • Using filters and visual comparisons to make patterns easier to investigate.
  • Communicating analytical outputs in a concise, stakeholder-friendly format.
